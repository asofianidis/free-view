# FreeView Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 23, 2026_

FreeView is a browser extension that replaces your new tab page with a visual speed dial. This policy explains what data FreeView handles and where it goes.

## Summary

FreeView has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads. Everything it stores stays in your browser. The developer never receives any of your data.

## Data FreeView handles

All of the following is stored only in your browser's local extension storage on your device:

- **Dials and groups** you create: page addresses, titles, colors and images you choose.
- **Page screenshots** of sites you have saved as dials, captured when you visit them or when you ask for a refresh. Screenshots are only taken of pages that are already dials.
- **Visit counts** for your dials (how often and when you opened them), shown in the Analytics section. You can turn this off or reset it in Settings.
- **Settings**, including an optional password lock. The password itself is never stored; only a salted PBKDF2 hash is kept.
- **Backups** of your dials and settings, created automatically or on request.

FreeView also reads your bookmarks, most visited sites, recently closed tabs, browsing history and open tabs, only to display them in the sidebar and in the import tools when you open them. This information is not copied, stored or sent anywhere.

## Data that leaves your device

Only in these cases, all of which you control:

- **Browser sync (off by default).** If you turn on sync, your dials, groups and settings are stored in your browser's built-in sync storage so they appear on your other computers. That data is handled by your browser vendor (for example Google for Chrome) under their privacy policy. Screenshots and images are never synced.
- **"Photo of the day" background (off by default).** If you choose it, background images are loaded from picsum.photos, which sees your IP address like any website you visit.
- **Google Fonts (off by default).** If you enter a Google Font name, the font is loaded from fonts.googleapis.com.
- **Export.** If you export a backup, the file is saved where you choose. FreeView does not upload it.

## What FreeView does not do

- It does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone.
- It does not use your data for advertising, credit scoring or any purpose unrelated to the speed dial.
- It does not run remote code.
- It does not track you across websites.

## Removing your data

Uninstalling FreeView deletes all of its local data. You can also delete everything from **Settings → Sync & backup → Delete all dials and settings**. If you used sync, turning sync off and uninstalling removes the synced copy from your browser account.
